# Hall of Boards privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Hall of Boards is a collection of board games made by Agus Kresna ("we"). Contact:
[agus.kresna.incorporated@gmail.com](mailto:agus.kresna.incorporated@gmail.com).

## The short version

- Hall of Boards **collects no personal data**. There are no accounts, no
  analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no tracking.
- The app **does not use the internet**. It asks for no permissions.
- Everything it saves stays on your device.

## What the app saves on your device

- The game in progress, so you can continue later.
- Finished games, for History, replays and stats (the last 100 games).
- Your last game setup, including any player names you typed.
- Your settings (language, theme, sound and so on).

You can delete finished games with **Settings > Reset stats**. Uninstalling the
app deletes everything.

## Device backup

If you use your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone
and iPad), it may include the app's saved games and settings, as for any other
app. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under your account, not by us.
You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Children

The app has no chat, no online play, no purchases and no data collection, so
it is safe for players of any age.

## Online play in the future

Online play may be added later. If it is, this policy will be updated
**before** that version is released, to explain exactly what is sent and
why. The current version sends nothing.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
